A note for grown-ups

Inspired by collecting objects by likeness and sorting everyday objects. The four modes move from noticing a shared attribute to applying a rule to a new item. Each mode has six puzzles. Read object names together, or use the speaker button for prompts and choices when your browser supports speech.

Before showing choices, ask your child to describe what they see. The choices use one intended rule, but a different explanation can be thoughtful too. Ask “What makes you think that?” and use “We found another way!” to discuss an alternative and move on. There are no lost points or time limits.

Keep exploring away from the screen: collect safe household items, sort them two ways, and invite your child to invent a rule for you to guess.
